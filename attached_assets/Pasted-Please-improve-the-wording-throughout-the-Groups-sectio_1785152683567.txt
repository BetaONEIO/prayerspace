Please improve the wording throughout the "Groups" section to make it immediately obvious that these are **Prayer Groups**, not generic social groups.

## Rename the section

Change the main navigation label from:

- Groups

to:

- Prayer Groups

Update any related page titles and headings to remain consistent throughout the app.

## Update headings

Change:

- My Groups
- Community

To:

- My Prayer Groups
- Community Prayer Groups

(Or simply keep "Community" if space is limited, but ensure the context still clearly indicates these are Prayer Groups.)

## Update introductory text

Replace:

"Prayer groups are focused spaces within your community — for deeper, more personal prayer with a trusted circle."

With something warmer and clearer, such as:

"Prayer Groups are private spaces where trusted friends, family and church members can pray together, encourage one another and share prayer updates."

or

"Pray together in trusted groups with friends, family and your church community."

## Update action cards

Change:

- Create a new group
- Join a group

To:

- Create Prayer Group
- Join Prayer Group

These labels should clearly communicate the purpose without requiring users to infer what type of group they are joining.

## Update terminology throughout the app

Search the entire application and replace generic references to "Group" with "Prayer Group" wherever appropriate, including:

- Buttons
- Empty states
- Confirmation messages
- Toast notifications
- Dialogues
- Forms
- Success messages
- Error messages
- Search placeholders
- Settings
- Notifications

Examples:

"Group created successfully"
→ "Prayer Group created successfully"

"Join Group"
→ "Join Prayer Group"

"Create Group"
→ "Create Prayer Group"

"My Groups"
→ "My Prayer Groups"

## Keep wording natural

Avoid repeating "Prayer Group" excessively where it feels unnecessary. Use shorter wording only where the surrounding context already makes it obvious.

The goal is that a first-time user instantly understands these are spaces specifically designed for praying together, rather than generic chat or community groups.
