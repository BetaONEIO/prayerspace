Please redesign this screen now that Prayer Communities include a Free plan.

The purpose of this screen is no longer to sell a subscription immediately. Instead, it should introduce Prayer Communities, explain their benefits, and encourage users to start with the Free Community plan.

## Page Content

Heading:
**Start a Prayer Community**

Supporting text:

Bring your church, ministry, family or prayer group together in one private space to pray, encourage one another, and stay connected through prayer.

## Feature List

Replace the existing feature section with something like:

**Private Community**
Only invited members can view your community, prayer requests and discussions.

**Invite Members**
Invite your church, Bible study, family or prayer group in seconds.

**Prayer Requests**
Share prayer requests, praise reports and answered prayers together.

**Group Chat**
Encourage one another through private conversations and prayer discussions.

**Prayer Notifications**
Receive notifications when new prayer requests, praise reports or important community updates are shared.

## Start Free Section

Replace the pricing summary with a Free Community message.

Title:
**Start Free**

Supporting text:

Create one Prayer Community with up to **10 members** completely free.

Upgrade at any time as your community grows.

## Buttons

Primary button:

**Create Free Community**

Secondary action:

**View Community Plans**

Selecting "View Community Plans" should navigate to the existing Community pricing page where users can compare all paid Community plans.

## User Experience

This screen should feel like an introduction to the feature rather than a paywall.

Users should be encouraged to experience Prayer Communities before making any purchasing decision.

The Free Community should be the default recommendation.

## Icons & Visual Style

Do not use standard Unicode emojis anywhere on this screen.

Instead, reuse Prayer Space's existing icon set and design language.

Icons should:

- Match the current Prayer Space icon style.
- Use the existing colour palette.
- Match the sizing, weight and spacing used throughout the app.
- Feel clean, modern and premium.
- Reuse existing Prayer Space icons wherever possible rather than creating new ones.

The goal is for this screen to feel like a seamless extension of the existing Prayer Space design system.

## Overall Goal

This screen should answer one simple question:

**"Why should I create a Prayer Community?"**

The user should leave this screen understanding the value of Prayer Communities and feel confident creating one for free, with a clear upgrade path available later as their church, ministry or prayer group grows.
